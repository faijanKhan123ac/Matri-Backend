🌸 About Medico App

Pregnancy Healthcare Portal — Complete Documentation

Medico App ek AI-powered pregnancy healthcare portal hai jo pregnant mahilaon ko ghar baithe world-class medical support provide karta hai. Isme AI doctor, video consultation, medicine store, aur kai unique health tools shamil hain — sab ek hi platform pe.

01 — AI DOCTOR

💗 Dr. Priya — AI Pregnancy Doctor

Dr. Priya ek AI-powered lady doctor avatar hai jo Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API se powered hai. Yeh lip sync karti hai, voice mein respond karti hai aur Hindi/English dono languages mein baat kar sakti hai. Pregnancy ke kisi bhi sawaal ka jawab deti hai — morning sickness se lekar labour signs tak — 24 ghante, 7 din.

Topics covered: Symptoms, diet, safe medicines, prenatal tests, baby development, emergency signs, trimester guide aur bahut kuch.
02 — VIDEO CONSULTATION

📹 Doctor se Video Call

Patient aur doctor WebRTC technology se real-time video call kar sakte hain. Patient join karta hai toh doctor ko automatically email notification aur in-app popup milta hai. Dono ko ek Room ID share karna hota hai. Call mein mic on/off, camera on/off aur in-call chat bhi available hai.

Technology: WebRTC (P2P), STOMP WebSocket, SockJS — same WiFi network pe best performance.
03 — APPOINTMENT BOOKING

📅 Doctor se Appointment Book Karo

Koi bhi registered doctor ko date aur time dekar appointment book kar sakte hain. Booking ke saath ₹100 consultation fees online pay kar sakte hain — Cash on Delivery, UPI ya Credit/Debit Card se. Medical reports aur photos bhi appointment ke saath upload ho sakti hain (max 5MB).

Payment options: COD (Pay at Visit) · UPI · Credit/Debit Card — sabka transaction ID generate hota hai.
04 — MEDICINE STORE

💊 Pregnancy Medicine E-Commerce

15 se zyada pregnancy-safe medicines available hain — Folic Acid, Iron, Calcium, Vitamin D3, Paracetamol, Omega-3, Magnesium aur bahut kuch. Category filter aur search se medicine dhundho, cart mein add karo, address fill karo aur order place karo. COD, UPI aur Card se payment hoti hai.

Categories: Vitamins · Pain & Fever · Digestive · Bone Health · Nausea Relief · Hormonal · Allergy · Diagnostics
05 — PREGNANCY WEEK TRACKER

🤰 Week-by-Week Baby Development

Apna pregnancy week enter karo aur janio baby ka size (fruits se compare karke), development milestones, weekly health tips aur common symptoms. Week 1 se Week 40 tak complete guide available hai. Ek visual progress bar dikhata hai pregnancy kitni complete hui hai. Dr. Priya se directly us week ke baare mein bhi poochh sakte hain.

06 — FETAL KICK COUNTER

👶 Baby Movement Tracker

28 weeks ke baad baby ki har movement "TAP" button se count karo. Session timer automatically chalta hai. Agar 2 ghante mein 10 kicks nahi aate toh automatic warning alert aata hai — yeh medically important hai. Pichle 7 din ka kick history locally save hota hai.

Medical Note: Normal range 28+ weeks mein 2 ghante mein kam se kam 10 movements honi chahiye. Kam hone pe turant doctor se milein.
07 — BABY HEARTBEAT SIMULATOR

💓 Relaxation & Bonding Tool

Web Audio API se baby ki heartbeat simulate kari jaati hai — koi external audio file nahi chahiye. BPM 110 se 180 tak adjust kar sakte hain (normal range 120-160). Char sound types available hain: Baby Heartbeat, Womb Sounds, Lullaby Tones aur Nature Calm. ECG-style waveform animation bhi dikhti hai. Sleep timer 5 se 30 minutes tak set kar sakte hain.

08 — PPD MENTAL HEALTH SCREENER

🧠 Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale

Postpartum Depression (prasav ke baad depression) screen karne ke liye internationally validated Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) tool implement kiya gaya hai. 10 questions, score 0-30. Score 10 se zyada hone pe doctor se milne ki salah di jaati hai. Hindi aur English dono languages mein available hai. Yeh sirf screening tool hai, medical diagnosis nahi.

09 — EMERGENCY SOS

🚨 One-Click Emergency Alert

Labor ya kisi bhi pregnancy emergency mein ek button click se registered family members ko email alert jaata hai jisme patient ki GPS location ka Google Maps link bhi hota hai. Multiple family members (naam, relation, email, phone) add kiye ja sakte hain. Alert mein patient ka naam, location aur hospital details hoti hain.

10 — DOCTOR FINDER

📍 Nearby Gynecologist Search

GPS location use karke aaspaas ke OB/GYN aur prenatal doctors dhundhe ja sakte hain. Interactive Leaflet.js map pe doctors ke markers dikhte hain. Doctor ka naam, hospital, specialization, phone number aur distance sab map pe available hota hai. Seedha map se appointment book ki ja sakti hai.
